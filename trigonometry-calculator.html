What the trigonometry calculator does

This calculator works with the sides and acute angles of a right-angled triangle. It can find the sine, cosine and tangent of an angle, complete a triangle when enough information is supplied, and show the remaining side lengths and angle. It is designed for school mathematics and introductory physics problems involving right triangles.

How to use it

  1. To calculate trigonometric ratios, enter an acute angle and choose degrees or radians.
  2. To solve a triangle from an angle and one side, enter the angle, enter the known side length and select whether that side is opposite, adjacent or the hypotenuse.
  3. Alternatively, enter any two side lengths in the opposite, adjacent and hypotenuse fields.
  4. Read the ratios and any completed triangle measurements in the result panel.

SOH CAH TOA and the main formulas

For an acute angle θ in a right-angled triangle, the sine ratio is opposite ÷ hypotenuse, the cosine ratio is adjacent ÷ hypotenuse, and the tangent ratio is opposite ÷ adjacent. The memory aid SOH CAH TOA summarises these relationships. Pythagoras’ theorem states that opposite² + adjacent² = hypotenuse².

  • sin θ = opposite / hypotenuse
  • cos θ = adjacent / hypotenuse
  • tan θ = opposite / adjacent
  • hypotenuse² = opposite² + adjacent²

Worked examples

Finding a ratio

If θ = 30°, sin 30° = 0.5, cos 30° ≈ 0.8660 and tan 30° ≈ 0.5774. These are ratios, so they have no unit.

Finding a missing side

A right triangle has an angle of 30° and a hypotenuse of 10 cm. The opposite side is 10 × sin 30° = 5 cm. The adjacent side is 10 × cos 30° ≈ 8.6603 cm.

Using Pythagoras

If the opposite and adjacent sides are 3 cm and 4 cm, the hypotenuse is √(3² + 4²) = 5 cm.

Degrees and radians

Degrees divide a full turn into 360 parts. Radians measure an angle using the radius of a circle; a full turn is 2π radians and 180° equals π radians. Make sure the selected unit matches the value entered. A calculator set to radians will interpret 30 as 30 radians, not 30 degrees.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • The hypotenuse is always opposite the 90° angle and is the longest side.
  • Opposite and adjacent are named relative to the angle being considered.
  • The calculator handles right triangles and acute angles, not arbitrary oblique triangles.
  • Side lengths must be positive, and the hypotenuse must be longer than either shorter side.
  • Displayed values are rounded. Keep extra digits during intermediate calculations.

Frequently asked questions

What does SOH CAH TOA mean?

It reminds you that Sine is Opposite over Hypotenuse, Cosine is Adjacent over Hypotenuse, and Tangent is Opposite over Adjacent.

Can I calculate an angle from two sides?

Yes. Enter two valid side lengths. The calculator uses inverse trigonometric relationships to find an acute angle.

Why must the angle be less than 90 degrees?

The tool is designed for the acute angles inside a right-angled triangle. The right angle itself is 90 degrees.
