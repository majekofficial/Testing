Counting the days between two dates

How long until the deadline? How many days did the project take? How long is the lease? This calculator answers with a single number: the days between a start date and an end date. Pick both dates and the difference appears, and it does not matter which date you enter first.

How the counting works

The calculator counts calendar days from the start date to the end date, including leap days. It does not count the start day itself, so two consecutive dates are 1 day apart.

days between = end date − start date

If your task needs both the first and last day to count, such as leave or an inclusive booking, add 1 to the result.

Worked examples

A whole year

From 1 January 2025 to 31 December 2025.

364 days

That is one day short of 365 because the start day is not counted. Counting both ends would give 365.

A leap year

From 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2024 gives 365 days, since 2024 has a 29 February.

Leap day in the middle

From 28 February to 1 March is 2 days in 2024 but 1 day in 2023, because 2024 has 29 February in between.

Converting to weeks

From 1 March 2026 to 25 December 2026 is 299 days. Divide by 7 to get 42 weeks and 5 days.

Beyond calendar days

  • Working days. A rough estimate is the number of weeks times 5. Public holidays must be removed by hand.
  • Months. Months differ in length, so the calculator reports days. For a person's age in years, months and days, use the Age Calculator.
  • Hours and minutes. For the time between two clock times, use the Time Duration Calculator.

Common mistakes

  • Forgetting the inclusive-count rule when a form asks for the number of days including both the first and last.
  • Mixing up day and month in the date picker, especially for dates such as 04/05.
  • Treating the days between as business days.

To count down from today instead, use the Countdown Calculator. For planning savings across the same period, see the Savings Goal Calculator.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include the start date?

No. It counts the days after the start date up to and including the end date. Add 1 if you need both ends counted.

Does it handle leap years?

Yes. Leap days are part of the calendar, so the results are exact.

Can the end date be before the start date?

Yes. The result is the number of days between them, shown as a positive number.

Are weekends excluded?

No. It is a calendar-day calculation.
