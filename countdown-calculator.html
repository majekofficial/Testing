Counting the days to an exam, trip or deadline

Choose a future date and the calculator tells you how many days remain. It is useful for exams, travel, applications, birthdays and anything else that sits on the calendar and benefits from a visible number.

How the days are counted

The calculator measures from the current moment on your device to the end of the target date (11:59 pm) and rounds up to whole days. The target day therefore counts as a day remaining, and the number changes when your local date changes. If you choose a date in the past, the result reads as a number of days ago.

Worked examples

A date ten days ahead

On 1 June, with a target of 11 June:

11 days remaining

The whole of 11 June is counted. The plain calendar difference would be 10 days, which is what the Date Calculator shows.

On the day itself

On 11 June with a target of 11 June the result is 1 days remaining, because the target date has not finished. The wording is a plain count, so “1 days” appears as shown.

Across the new year

On 30 December with a target of 2 January the result is 4 days remaining: 31 December, 1 January and 2 January plus the rest of today.

A date in the past

Choosing 1 November when today is 12 November gives 10 days ago.

Turning a countdown into a plan

  • Divide the days. If 44 days remain and you have 4 subjects to revise, that is 11 days each. The Study Time Calculator splits time you have across subjects.
  • Work backwards from the deadline. Set interim dates, such as a draft two weeks before submission.
  • Add buffer days. Plans slip. Aim to finish a few days early.
  • Check time zones. The calculator uses your device date. An event in another time zone may start earlier or later in real terms.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing the wrong year, especially near December and January.
  • Forgetting that the target day counts, which makes the result one higher than a simple date difference.
  • Using a date-only countdown for an event with a specific start hour, since the hours are not shown.

To work out your age to the day, see the Age Calculator. For savings towards a dated goal, the Savings Goal Calculator turns the months left into a monthly amount.

Frequently asked questions

Does the countdown include today?

It counts through the end of the target date, so the target day itself is included, and any remaining part of today is counted as a day.

Can I count down to a date next year?

Yes. Select the full date including the year.

What does “days ago” mean?

The target date has passed. The number shows how many days ago it was.

Does it show hours and minutes?

No. The result is in whole days.
