Work with two points on a coordinate plane

The Coordinate Geometry Calculator analyses the straight line segment joining two points. Enter the x- and y-coordinates of each point to find the distance between them, the midpoint, the slope and the equation of the line. It is useful for coordinate geometry exercises, graph work and checking solutions.

How to use it

  1. Enter x₁ and y₁ for the first point A.
  2. Enter x₂ and y₂ for the second point B.
  3. Calculate to see the distance, midpoint, slope and line equation.

Formulas used

  • Distance: d = √[(x₂ − x₁)² + (y₂ − y₁)²]
  • Midpoint: M = ((x₁ + x₂)/2, (y₁ + y₂)/2)
  • Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁), when x₂ is not equal to x₁.
  • Line equation: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁).

Worked example

Take A(2, 3) and B(6, 11). The change in x is 6 − 2 = 4 and the change in y is 11 − 3 = 8. The distance is √(4² + 8²) = √80 ≈ 8.944. The midpoint is ((2 + 6)/2, (3 + 11)/2) = (4, 7). The slope is 8/4 = 2, so the line equation is y = 2x − 1.

Interpreting slope

A positive slope rises from left to right. A negative slope falls from left to right. A zero slope describes a horizontal line. A vertical line has an undefined slope because its change in x is zero, so division by zero would be required. The calculator reports this case rather than displaying a misleading number.

Common mistakes and limitations

  • Keep each x-coordinate paired with its matching y-coordinate.
  • Subtract in the same order in both parts of the slope formula.
  • Distance is never negative, even when coordinate differences are negative.
  • When both points are identical, the distance is zero and there is no unique line through two distinct points.
  • The line equation is shown in slope-intercept form when the line is not vertical.

Frequently asked questions

Can the slope be negative?

Yes. A negative slope means y decreases as x increases along the line.

Why is the slope of a vertical line undefined?

Its horizontal change is zero, so the slope formula would divide by zero.

What does the midpoint represent?

It is the point exactly halfway between the two given endpoints.
