What a logarithm tells you

A logarithm answers the question: to what power must the base be raised to give this number? If 23 = 8, then log2 8 = 3. This calculator takes a base and a number, returns the logarithm and, when the answer is a whole number or a simple fraction, confirms it as exact.

The definition and a useful rule

logb x = y means by = x

For a base that your calculator does not have a key for, use the change of base rule:

logb x = ln x ÷ ln b

Worked examples

Exact answers

log base 2 of 8 is 3, log base 10 of 1000 is 3, and log base 5 of 125 is 3. The calculator adds “This is exact” and shows the power check, for instance 5 to the power 3 = 125.

A negative logarithm

log base 2 of 1/4 is −2, because 2−2 = 1/4. Numbers between 0 and 1 have negative logarithms when the base is greater than 1.

An answer that is not whole

log base 3 of 20 is about 2.726833: between 2 and 3, because 3² = 9 and 3³ = 27. Check with the change of base rule: ln 20 ÷ ln 3 = 2.9957 ÷ 1.0986 ≈ 2.7268.

How many times can you halve it?

log base 2 of 1000 is about 9.965784. That is roughly how many times 1000 can be halved before reaching 1, and why 1,000 values need about 10 bits to label them.

Input limits

  • The base must be positive and not equal to 1, and the number must be positive. Anything else gives a dash.
  • The base must be a number, so it cannot be typed as e. For natural logarithms, use ln on the Scientific Calculator.
  • You can enter fractions such as 1/4 as the number.

Where logarithms appear

  • Growth and decay: solving 1.05n = 2 for n gives the doubling time of 5% growth, n = log 2 ÷ log 1.05 ≈ 14.2 years.
  • Scales: pH, decibels and the Richter scale are logarithmic.
  • Algebra: logarithms undo exponents, which makes equations like 3x = 20 solvable.

Common mistakes

  • Mixing up log and ln. On many calculators log means base 10 and ln means base e.
  • Trying to take a log of zero or a negative number.
  • Assuming log(a + b) = log a + log b. The correct rule is log(ab) = log a + log b.

To raise numbers to powers, use the Exponent Calculator. Logarithms also solve for the position of a term in a geometric sequence.

Frequently asked questions

What is the logarithm of 1?

Zero, for any valid base, because any base to the power 0 equals 1.

What is a natural logarithm?

A logarithm to base e (about 2.718). It is written ln and is available on the scientific calculator.

Why can I not take the log of a negative number?

No real power of a positive base gives a negative result, so the logarithm does not exist in the real numbers.

How do I find a log to any base on a basic calculator?

Divide the natural or base-10 log of the number by the same log of the base.
